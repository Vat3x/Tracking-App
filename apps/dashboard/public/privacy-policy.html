Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 12, 2026

LoadMind Tracker ("we", "our", "us") operates the LoadMind Tracker mobile application (the "App") for commercial truck drivers and the LoadMind Tracker web dashboard for dispatchers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Account Information: Phone number, name, and email address used to create your account.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates collected while you are on duty. Location is shared with your dispatcher approximately every 40 minutes. Location is only collected when you have consented to tracking by joining a company.
  • Device Information: Battery level, device speed, and push notification tokens for delivering trip notifications.
  • Trip Data: Trip origin, destination, stops, and status information assigned by your dispatcher.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account.
  • To share your real-time location with your assigned dispatcher while you are on duty.
  • To deliver trip assignments and notifications.
  • To display trip routes and estimated arrival times.
  • To maintain location history for your dispatcher to review.

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only with:

  • Your Dispatcher/Company: Your location, battery level, speed, and trip status are visible to dispatchers in your assigned company.
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, and notifications. We use Google Maps for map display and routing.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Location Data

Location tracking is consent-based. You must accept an invitation from a dispatcher to join a company before any location data is collected. Location data is collected in the background while you are on duty and the app is running. You can stop sharing your location at any time by going offline.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Location history is retained for operational purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase Authentication, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and Firestore security rules to protect your data. Access to your data is restricted to authorized dispatchers in your company.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App or on our website.
