Privacy Policy

LoadMind Tracker

LoadMind Tracker ("we", "our", or "the App") is a fleet tracking platform operated by LoadMind. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address or phone number, and authentication credentials.

Location Data: The driver app collects GPS coordinates periodically (approximately every 40 minutes) while tracking is active. Location data includes latitude, longitude, speed, and heading.

Device Information: We collect battery level and charging status to display on the dispatcher dashboard.

Push Notification Tokens: We store device tokens to send trip notifications (new trips, status updates, location edits).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Display real-time driver locations to dispatchers within the same company
  • Send trip assignments and status notifications
  • Provide shareable tracking links so customers can view delivery ETA
  • Maintain location history for trip records

3. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only with:

  • Your dispatcher/company: Dispatchers in your assigned company can see your location, battery status, and trip history
  • Tracking link recipients: When a dispatcher creates a tracking link for a trip, the recipient can see the assigned driver's live location and route for that specific trip
  • Service providers: We use Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and push notifications. We use Google Maps for map display in the driver app

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Realtime Database, and Authentication) with encryption in transit and at rest. Access to driver data requires authentication and company membership verification.

5. Enterprise Monitoring Disclosure

LoadMind Tracker is designed exclusively for enterprise fleet management — specifically for commercial drivers who are employed by or contracted with a logistics/transportation company. The app enables employers (dispatchers) to monitor the location of their drivers during working hours for legitimate business purposes such as route coordination and delivery management.

This app is not designed or intended for monitoring spouses, family members, or any individual outside of an established employer-employee or dispatcher-driver relationship. Use of this app to track individuals without a legitimate commercial relationship is prohibited.

Drivers are always aware they are being monitored:

  • Drivers must accept an invite from their company before any tracking begins
  • Drivers explicitly go "online" to start sharing their location
  • A persistent notification is displayed at all times while location sharing is active, clearly identifying the app and the tracking behavior
  • Drivers can stop sharing at any time by toggling offline or revoking location permissions

6. Location Tracking Consent & Background Access

Location tracking requires your explicit consent through device permissions. The app requests background location access ("Allow all the time") so that location updates continue when the app is minimized or the screen is off. This access is disclosed to the driver before the permission is requested.

A persistent foreground service notification ("Sharing your location with your dispatcher") is shown at all times while background tracking is active. You can disable location permissions at any time through your device settings. Tracking only occurs while the driver has toggled themselves "online" in the app.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Location history is kept for operational and record-keeping purposes. When you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently removed (see below).

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your profile and trip data within the app
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account. This removes your profile, location history, and cancels any active trips
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at team@load-mind.com.
